## AI Agent Types

### Simple Reflex (Reacts)

The most simple type of AI Agent and it follows predefined rules to make decisions. This can be interpreted as Condition-Action rules.

Better fit for predictiable environments with a clear set of rules and do not adapt well to new situations.

### Model-Based Reflex (Remembers)

It can modify its understanding of the environment by tracking information in a state and being able to modify this state base on new conditions or by how its own actions affect the environment.

### Goal-based (Aims)

Use internal models to simulate future scenarios to determine the actions that will lead to the expected output.

### Utility-based (Evaluates)

It ranks options for future outputs based on how "satistifying" each future output will turn out to be.

### Learning (Improves)

It updates its behavior based on outcomes from performed actions and this produces "learning".
